# Campus Screen

## Purpose
A routing hub for campus-wide community discovery.

## Route
`/campus` (Default redirects to `/campus/discover`)

## Information Hierarchy
- **Header**: "Campus"
- **Sub-navigation**: 
  - Discover (Events)
  - Leaderboard
  - Clubs
- **Content Area**: Renders the active sub-navigation screen.

## Actions
- Switch tabs to navigate between the community features.
